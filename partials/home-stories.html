योगश्चित्तवृत्तिनिरोधः

Yogaś citta-vṛtti-nirodhaḥ

Yoga is the stilling of the fluctuations of the mind.

— Patanjali, Yoga Sutras

उद्धरेदात्मनात्मानं नात्मानमवसादयेत्

Uddhared ātmanātmānaṁ nātmānam avasādayet

Let a person lift themselves by their own self; let them not lower themselves.

— The Bhagavad Gita

यथा दीपो निवातस्थो नेङ्गते सोपमा स्मृता

Yathā dīpo nivāta-stho neṅgate sopamā smṛtā

As a lamp sheltered from the wind does not flicker, so is the steady mind of the yogi.

— The Bhagavad Gita

तदा द्रष्टुः स्वरूपेऽवस्थानम्

Tadā draṣṭuḥ svarūpe 'vasthānam

Then pure awareness is established in itself.

— Patanjali, Yoga Sutras
